GCash Die-Cut Brochure, just in time for the holiday season.

A holiday-inspired die-cut brochure created for GCash, designed to promote app downloads and digital vouchers in a fun and interactive way. The project combines festive visuals, custom folds, and mascot-centered branding to create an engaging print experience while maintaining GCash’s recognizable identity.

The Challenge

Visually appealing and functional.

The brochure utilized a custom die-cut structure, careful consideration had to be given to panel alignment, spacing, and the placement of important elements such as the QR code, voucher sections, and calls-to-action to maintain readability after printing and assembly.

Another challenge was incorporating festive holiday visuals while still preserving the recognizable branding and professional identity of GCash. The design needed to feel playful and engaging without compromising brand consistency.
